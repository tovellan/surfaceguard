# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Project participants must provide a respectful, professional, and harassment-free environment regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical choices and evidence, not personal attributes.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while protecting security boundaries.
- Respect privacy and coordinated disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions and appeal them through the documented governance process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, impersonation, doxxing, and publication of another party's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private repository security advisory marked as a conduct report. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban participation. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
